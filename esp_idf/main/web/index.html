Settings

A box config in JSON: name, location, mqtt, hblink, cc, channels and radios, plus run: the radios this board runs (a name or a list; two roamers or repeaters and one SDR at most; without it, the first roamer or repeater, else the first SDR). "enabled": false on a radio leaves it out (run may still name it). For the roamer, on the box or on each radio: modem (usb / uart), uart {tx, rx, num (1 or 2)}, speed; on a radio, usb {serial, port} to tell two on a hub apart; oled {sda, scl}. In the SDR's section: usb {transfers, transfer_kb}, dsp_cores (1 or 2); its sample_rate is 288000 or 240000 here. A roamer on USB and an SDR both run: whichever is plugged in runs, unless "usb_hub": true. Comments are fine. It's checked before it's saved. The Wi-Fi networks and the NTP servers show as wifi {networks} and ntp {servers, poll_s}: passwords never leave the board, so a network without a "password" keeps the one it has (add "password" to set one).

SDR tests

expected output (no dongle needed). The USB spike measures how much of the RTL-SDR's stream Full Speed USB delivers at 288,
250 and 240 kS/s (the dongle must be in). Both restart the board; the results show here and in the log.
