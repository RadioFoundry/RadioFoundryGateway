Settings

A box config in JSON, version 3: name, id, callsign, location, defaults, devices, frequencies, apps, networks and policies. Every unit it makes runs: a repeater app, an SFR or DCDM frequency, a device that transmits on simplex frequencies, an SDR that hears; two units that transmit and one SDR at most. On a modem: type, port {uart, tx, rx} or {usb, serial, usb_port} (to tell two on a hub apart), hears and transmits. On the SDR: driver, rx {rate, gain_db}, usb {transfers, transfer_kb}, dsp_cores (1 or 2); its rate is 288000 or 240000 here. At the top: oled {sda, scl}. A modem on USB and an SDR both run: whichever is plugged in runs, unless "usb_hub": true. Comments are fine. It's checked before it's saved. The Wi-Fi networks and the NTP servers show as wifi {networks} and ntp {servers, poll_s}: passwords never leave the board, so a network without a "password" keeps the one it has (add "password" to set one).

SDR tests


The self-test runs a built-in 0.6 s recording through the SDR receiver and checks it against the
expected output (no dongle needed). The USB spike measures how much of the RTL-SDR's stream Full Speed USB delivers at 288,
250 and 240 kS/s (the dongle must be in). Both restart the board; the results show here and in the log.
